# Active Goal Tracking

Without target metrics, knowledge systems degenerate into disorganized note graveyards. **Active Goal Tracking** gives the cognitive system a clear target state to drive toward.

## Goal Metrics & Lifecycle

* **Progress Percentage:** Calculated based on completed subtasks and connected nodes.
* **Delta Evaluation:** At session startup, the system evaluates what changed since the last session toward achieving the goal.

## System Convergence

Active goals bind together [[01_cognitive_architectures]], [[02_knowledge_graphs]], and [[03_procedural_skills]] into a single, cohesive intelligence loop focused on measurable execution.
