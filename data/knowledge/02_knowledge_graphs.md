# Knowledge Graphs in AI

A **Knowledge Graph** structures information as a network of nodes (entities) and edges (relationships). In local-first personal knowledge management (PKM), it turns isolated notes into an interconnected neural network.

## Benefits for LLM Reasoning

* **Deterministic Context:** Reduces AI hallucinations by feeding grounded, explicit facts.
* **Bi-directional Linking:** Navigating via `[[wikilinks]]` allows agents to traverse multi-hop logical relationships.
* **Structural Health:** Identifying orphan nodes helps maintain system hygiene.

## Integration

Knowledge graphs provide the structural semantic memory required by [[01_cognitive_architectures]]. They work hand-in-hand with [[03_procedural_skills]] to execute complex workflows against specific [[04_active_goals]].
